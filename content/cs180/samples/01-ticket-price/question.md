# Ticket price

Write a program in `Main.java` that works out the price of a cinema ticket.

Print `Enter your age:` on its own line, then read the customer's age as a whole number on the next line.
Print `Are you a student? (y/n)` on its own line, then read the answer on the next line.

| Customer | Price |
|---|---|
| Under 12 | $6.50 |
| 65 or over | $7.25 |
| Anyone else who answers `y` or `Y` | $8.75 |
| Everyone else | $11.00 |

Then print `Ticket price: $` followed by the price with exactly two decimals.

## Sample run

Input is shown here for clarity; your program does not print it.

```
Enter your age:
20
Are you a student? (y/n)
y
Ticket price: $8.75
```

The sample run is one of the tests. The rest are hidden.
